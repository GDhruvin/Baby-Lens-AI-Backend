Privacy Policy for BabyLens AI

Effective Date: July 31, 2026 Last Updated: September 23, 2026 App: BabyLens: AI Baby Photoshoots
🔒 Our Commitment: We never sell your personal data or uploaded baby photos. Photos are processed solely to create requested themes.

Welcome to BabyLens AI ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and safeguarding the personal data of your family and children. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, process, disclose, and secure your information when you use our mobile application BabyLens: AI Baby Photoshoots (the "App") and website.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information You Voluntarily Provide

  • Account Credentials: Name, email address, profile photo, and unique authentication identifier provided via Google Sign-In or Firebase Authentication.
  • Uploaded Photos: Photographs of your baby, toddler, or child that you choose to upload for theme generation.
  • Baby Profile Details: Voluntary baby profile details such as name, age/birthdate, and gender to personalize themes.

B. Automatically Collected Technical Data

  • Device Identifiers & Diagnostics: Anonymous hardware device model, operating system version, and anonymized crash logs used solely for performance monitoring and bug fixing.

2. Generative AI & Facial Data Disclosure

Because BabyLens AI utilizes generative artificial intelligence to produce themed portraits, we maintain strict privacy policies regarding facial imagery:

  • Single-Purpose Processing: Uploaded baby photos and facial geometry are used strictly and transiently to match facial likeness onto requested digital photoshoot themes.
  • No Biometric Identification: We do NOT use uploaded images or facial data for biometric surveillance, face recognition databases, or cross-service identity matching.
  • No Public Model Training: Your child's photos and generated outputs are never used to train public foundational AI models.
  • Zero Commercial Sale: We strictly never sell, license, rent, or trade your baby photos to third-party data brokers or advertisers.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To process images and deliver personalized, studio-grade AI baby photoshoots.
  • To maintain your secure private gallery and synchronize purchased photoshoot credits.
  • To authenticate your session and prevent fraudulent purchases via Google Play Billing.
  • To provide customer support when requested by parents.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

BabyLens AI is designed exclusively for use by parents, legal guardians, and adults aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly solicit or collect data directly from children without verifiable parental consent. All photographs of minors must be provided exclusively by their parent or legal guardian.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You have the absolute right to delete your account, baby profiles, and all uploaded/generated imagery at any time:

  • In-App Deletion: Open the app, navigate to Profile → Delete Account, and confirm. All cloud storage photos, MongoDB database records, baby profiles, and Firebase authentication credentials will be permanently purged immediately.
  • Web Deletion Request: If you uninstalled the app or prefer web-based deletion, visit our Account & Data Deletion Request Page.

6. Third-Party Infrastructure

We work only with industry-standard, SOC2-compliant enterprise cloud infrastructure providers:

  • Google Firebase & Google Cloud: Secure database storage, authentication, and encrypted cloud asset hosting.
  • Google Play In-App Billing: Handles transaction processing securely. We never store or view your credit card details.
