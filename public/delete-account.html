Account & Data Deletion Request

Google Play & Apple Compliance Portal for BabyLens: AI Baby Photoshoots (Package: com.babylens)

At BabyLens AI, we strictly uphold your right to data sovereignty. You can delete your account and all associated child photos, profile details, and generated artworks permanently at any time.

📱 Method 1: Delete Directly Inside the Mobile App (Instant)

If you currently have the BabyLens AI app installed on your phone:

  1. Open the BabyLens AI app on your device.
  2. Tap the Profile tab in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the action in the security dialog by tapping Delete Forever.

✅ All cloud storage assets, database records, and authentication tokens will be deleted immediately.

🌐 Method 2: Request Web Deletion (If App is Uninstalled)

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot log in, you can request manual deletion by sending an email from the email address associated with your BabyLens account:

Recipient: support@babylens.app or babylens.ai@gmail.com
Subject Line: Account and Data Deletion Request - BabyLens
Processing Timeline: Requests submitted via email are verified and processed within 48 hours.
